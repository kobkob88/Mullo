# Security

Found a weakness in Mullo? Please tell us privately, not in a public issue.

- Email **hello@lizai.io** with the subject **Security**.
- Say what you found, how to reproduce it, and what someone could do with it.
- Please don't access or change anyone else's notes, and don't run tests that slow the service
  down for other people.

We answer within 7 days and tell you when it is fixed. The same address is in
`https://mullo.lizai.io/.well-known/security.txt`.

**In scope:** the Worker (`worker/`), the AI bridge (`bridge/`), the web app (`app/`), the Android,
Wear OS, iPhone and Apple Watch apps (`android/`, `ios/`), and the database rules (`sql/`).

**Not in scope:** reports from automated scanners without a working example, missing headers with
no real effect, and anything that needs a person's unlocked phone.
